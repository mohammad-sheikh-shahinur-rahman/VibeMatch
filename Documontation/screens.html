⚠️ Important Third-Party Services Disclaimer & Usage Costs

Please Note Before Deployment: VibeMatch relies on external third-party service providers to deliver enterprise-grade performance, scalable databases, push messaging, and secure payment processing. These services are operated by independent third-party companies:

  • MongoDB Database Hosting: (e.g. MongoDB Atlas or self-hosted VPS instance). Free tier is sufficient for testing; cloud hosting usage scales according to your database size and bandwidth.
  • Payment Gateways (Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay): Payment gateways charge standard payment processing fees per transaction according to their merchant terms.
  • Firebase (Google LLC): Used for Phone Authentication (SMS verification), Google Sign-In, and Push Notifications. Free tiers are provided by Google, with billing applicable if SMS thresholds are exceeded.
  • Apple App Store & Google Play Console: Publishing apps requires an Apple Developer Program membership ($99/year) and a Google Play Console developer account ($25 one-time). Standard app store commission applies to in-app purchases.
  • WebRTC TURN Servers: Public STUN servers are included for free. If you deploy for millions of users behind strict corporate firewalls, a commercial TURN provider (e.g. Twilio, Metered, Xirsys) may incur usage-based bandwidth fees.

The buyer / licensee is solely responsible for creating their own developer accounts, configuring their credentials, and covering any associated operational or usage costs incurred by external providers.
